Selected Work

Food Organizing System2024

Shared refrigerators can get chaotic and disorganized. This project aims to fix that problem. Early prototypes used off-the-shelf suction cups to mount the barrier to glass shelves, but they didn't hold up over time — so I designed my own mechanical suction cups for extra grip and durability.

PLA, Polypropylene, Rubber · Designing in Context Studio · Senior

Meditation Productivity App2023

A smart device for office workers. I explored several desk-ornament concepts for communicating with a companion meditation app, and landed on a piezo speaker design that plays impromptu guided-meditation audio while surfacing visual data from the app.

Conceptual Product · Dell User Experience · Intern

Convertible Decoration Seating2023–2024

Side chair that transforms into wall art to save space. The seat pan was originally a molded shell, but early prototypes weren't comfortable for a long sit — I switched to a felt pad, which added cushioning while keeping the low profile the design required.

Cedar, Poplar, Titanium, Oil Paint · Furniture Studio · Senior

Hands-On Educational Music Kit2023

Teaching the science of music to older elementary aged kids. What began as a build-your-own-instrument toy kit grew into something more — I added a pamphlet breaking down the basic science of how each instrument produces sound.

Wood, Cardboard, PVC · STEAM Box Studio · Senior

Analog Stylized Prosthetic2022

Linkages allow passive dynamic grip for transradial amputees. The palm element was originally a solid volume; after experimenting with a simpler planar profile, I found it performed the same function with less material and complexity.

Stainless Steel · Prosthetics Studio · Junior
